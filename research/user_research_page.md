## Column 3: Insights (grouped by affinity, 4 clusters → insight statement)

> 老师要求：根据问题把原始小贴纸进行分组，讨论的是同一件事，分 3-5 个组。
> 如何从不同人的观点找到共性来做出新的产品。

### Cluster 1: The Output Bottleneck
**"There's a chasm between thought and text."**

Sticky notes grouped here:
- P1: "Compress ideas into keywords, can't decode them later"
- P1: "If I can't distill it, I don't bother"
- P3: "Translating a vague idea into text is extremely difficult"
- P3: "Brain-computer interface — skip the output step"
- P4: "Words are pale, can't express 50%"
- P4: "Recording complex details quickly is nearly impossible"
- P5: "I read a book and have ten thoughts, but by the time I open my notes app, I can only remember two"

**→ Insight:** Current tools require users to articulate ideas *before* recording them. But the ideas that most need capturing are precisely the ones that can't yet be put into words.

### Cluster 2: Recording ≠ Retaining
**"Wrote it down, but it didn't actually stay."**

Sticky notes grouped here:
- P1: "Connections between words obvious at the time, gone on revisit"
- P1: "WeChat stacks vertically — can't see the throughline"
- P3: "Looked at it two days later, deleted it"
- P4: "WeChat file transfer — painful to scroll through endlessly"
- P5: "Notes scattered across five apps — they might as well not exist"
- P5: "The idea I lost was connected to something I read last month, and I'll never recover that link"

**→ Insight:** Even when users push through the bottleneck and record something, the note decays into noise. Without structure or context, a note from last Tuesday is indistinguishable from nothing.

### Cluster 3: AI Lacks Humanity
**"I don't know how to talk to it."**

Sticky notes grouped here:
- P3: "AI is too robotic — I don't know how to start"
- P3: "The more robotic, the less I want to talk — vicious cycle"
- P4: "AI logic is too perfect — lacks the flaws where beauty lives"
- P4: "Emotionally can't accept talking to a non-human about creation"
- P2: "AI advice is too simple for anything emotional"
- P5: "AI gives textbook answers — I don't want a lecture, I want someone to riff with me"

**→ Insight:** The default AI posture — safe, generic, logically flawless — creates a communication barrier for creative exploration. Users need an AI that feels like a thinking partner, not a customer service bot.

### Cluster 4: The Sacredness of Thinking
**"The act of thinking itself has meaning."**

Sticky notes grouped here:
- P4: "Thinking about things yourself — that act has meaning"
- P4: "Being indirect is part of being human"
- P4: "Adding illustrations during recording kills the feeling"
- P4: "Wants a 'field' — immersive, not a tool"

**→ Insight:** Not every creator wants AI to participate in their creative process — and that's a boundary worth respecting. The tool must amplify the user's thinking, never replace it.

## Column 4: Brainstorm (feature concepts, keyword style)

> 老师：关键词引导 ideation 画风

### From Cluster 1 → Output Bottleneck:
- **Conversation-first capture** — talk to AI, don't write to a blank page
- **Fragment-friendly input** — accept single words, no structure required
- **AI as translator** — turn spoken fragments into coherent structure

### From Cluster 2 → Recording ≠ Retaining:
- **Auto-generated idea cards** — structured snapshots from raw conversation
- **Thought timeline** — visual history of how an idea evolved
- **Semantic search** — find ideas by meaning, not exact keywords

### From Cluster 3 → AI Lacks Humanity:
- **Persona design** — give the AI a specific character, not generic assistant
- **Prompt for exploration** — curious, opinionated, never corrective
- **Anti-praise policy** — no "great idea!", no hollow validation

### From Cluster 4 → Sacredness of Thinking:
- **AI as amplifier, not author** — never generate the idea, only deepen it
- **Minimal UI during capture** — no distracting formatting, colors, categories
- **Opt-in depth** — user decides how far AI goes

---

## Column 5: Opportunities (3 design directions)

### Opportunity 1: Capture Without Friction
> Design a capture experience with zero articulation cost. The user starts with a single word or a rambling voice note — the AI handles the rest.

### Opportunity 2: AI as Co-conspirator
> Design a specialized AI persona for idea exploration — curious, opinionated, comfortable with ambiguity. Not a generic assistant, but a thinking partner with character.

### Opportunity 3: From Capture to Connection
> Design a system that doesn't just store individual ideas, but automatically discovers hidden relationships between them — turning a collection of notes into a living network of thought.

---

## Bottom Insight (spanning full width)

> While every participant shared a universal frustration — ideas that fade before they can be recorded — their needs diverge sharply in how much AI involvement they'll accept. This tension between **amplification** and **autonomy** became the central design constraint for Drawer.
